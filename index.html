Speech not detected? Try these
  • Use the latest Chrome, Edge or Safari. Firefox and iPhone/iPad browsers other than Safari can't check speech.
  • Allow microphone access when your browser asks. If you blocked it, re-enable it in the site settings and reload.
  • Speech checking uses your browser's online speech service, so you need an internet connection.
  • Choose the right microphone in your device's sound settings, use a quiet room, and speak one word clearly just after tapping.
  • Hosting the page on https (rather than opening the file directly) makes microphone permission more reliable.
  • Brand names from other languages are hard for any recogniser, so a near-miss is normal. Use “Record & compare” to judge by ear.
